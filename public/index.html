Privacy at a glance

  • Analysis, scoring, local optimization and cost estimates run in your browser and need no key or network.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere unless you click Count exactly or Improve with AI. Both go through your local server, which holds any API keys. Keys are never sent to the browser.
